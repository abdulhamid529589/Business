## 2. Category 1: Trump & America

### 2.1 Background Philosophy: René Girard

**Three Key People to Understand:**
- **René Girard** — French philosopher who taught in California for 30+ years; died in 2012
- **Peter Thiel** — Girard's disciple; Silicon Valley investor, founder of Palantir
- **JD Vance** — Thiel's disciple; US Vice President

**Mimetic Desire — What Is It?**
- All human beings desire roughly the same things
- When everyone wants the same thing → conflict is inevitable
- Example: A luxury apartment in South Mumbai — everyone wants it → fierce competition

**The Scapegoat Mechanism:**
- When people are fighting each other → a smart leader finds a Scapegoat
- The leader says: *"It's not your fault. The problem is that person over there. Sacrifice them and everything will be fine."*
- Hitler did this with Jews
- It happens in corporate life — "sack the CEO, share price will recover"
- It happens in cricket — "fire the coach, the team will win"

**Girard's most important book:** *"Things Hidden Since the Foundation of the World"*  
**Simplified version:** *"Wanting"* by Luke Burgis (also available as a comic book version)

### 4.4 The North India vs South India Sports Theory (Fascinating Framework)

**The puzzle:** Why are there no South Indian wrestling champions? Why are there no North Indian chess grandmasters?

| Region | Historical Context | Result |
|---|---|---|
| North India | Invaded every ~20 years — Mughals, Afghans, others — through the Afghanistan-Pakistan-plains route | High discount rate, focus on physical strength → Wrestling, martial arts |
| South India | Rarely invaded — even the deepest Mughal penetration barely reached Hyderabad; nobody reached Madurai | Low discount rate, long-term thinking → Chess, intellect, science |

**The Discount Rate Explanation:**
- If you don't know whether you'll be alive tomorrow → your discount rate is very high → you focus on today → physical training, rich food to fight invaders → no time for 10-year intellectual pursuits
- If that physical threat is absent → low discount rate → you can plan 10–15 years ahead → the life of the mind takes precedence

> *"I love both Madurai and Ludhiana — but they are chalk and cheese."*

## 6. AI's Double Effect: Substitution vs Complementarity

### 6.1 Substitution Effect — Happening Now (2026)

**Historical parallel:**
Electric light bulb arrives (1890) → **Candle makers are done.** Fast, brutal, irreversible.

**2026 AI Substitution Effect in India:**
- Employers: "We're paying for Microsoft Copilot and OpenAI. We can't also pay the same headcount."
- Large IT firms quietly laying off **tens of thousands**, replacing with AI
- Some have publicly stated: "We'll get double the work from half the people"
- White-collar jobs — coding, data analysis, call centers, basic legal work — all at serious risk

**India's unique vulnerability:**
- Our entire national business model was: "Young educated population will get jobs"
- AI is **directly attacking this model**
- White-collar job data already showing shrinkage in 2024–25; 2026 will see more

---

### 6.2 Complementarity Effect — Coming in 2027–28

**Historical parallel:**
Electric light bulb (1890) → Hollywood (1920), gramophone, pop stars, film stars — **30 years later**

**With AI:** Saurabh estimates 2–3 years (not 30) for complementarity to kick in.

**What the Complementarity phase looks like for India:**

Scenario: A hospital chain in Latvia wants to automate their customer service entirely. They have 10 years of call records.

1. They send the data to someone sitting at home in Madurai or Pune
2. That person **organizes, cleans, and labels** the data
3. A **bot trainer** then trains a Latvian AI bot on that data
4. The bot handles all hospital queries in Latvian

**India's role:** The LLMs (language models) will come from America. The **actual implementation work** — data cleaning, labeling, bot training, application building — will be done by India's cheap, educated, English-speaking population.

**Potential scale:**
- Up to **100 million gig jobs** could be created
- People sitting at home in Mulund, Malad, Koramangala — working for the world
- New Indian companies will emerge to coordinate this global gig workforce

**New company model:**
- DeepSeek = 200 people created a world-class LLM
- 500 smart people + AI = multi-billion dollar empire
- No need for giant office parks in Hinjewadi or Bengaluru's ORR

> *"India's rebirth will be around new gig jobs and small companies that use AI to create colossal empires."*

### 6.3 AI's Three Layers — Where to Invest

| Layer | Description | Saurabh's Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Chips** | The hardware that runs AI | Bought TSMC — makes chips for everyone, regardless of which chip design wins |

**The winner will be:**
Companies that combine proprietary data with AI to reinforce competitive advantage.

**Example:** Bajaj Finance → proprietary lending data + Microsoft AI → reinforcing dominance that no outsider can replicate.

## 7. Gold & Silver: What to Do?

### The Textbook Answer

| Asset | Long-term Return | Correlation with Others |
|---|---|---|
| US Equities | ~14–15% | Low with India, low with Gold |
| Indian Equities | ~10% | Low with US, low with Gold |

### Why Gold Has Rallied

- Central banks around the world are scared by the scale of global disruption
- They are buying gold with sovereign wealth — **fear-driven buying**
- This has jacked up gold prices dramatically

### Saurabh's Contrarian Take

> *"The worst of the fear trade may be behind us. Tariff drama has broadly peaked — barring India and Brazil. The Fed is still cutting rates. US earnings growth is running at 11–12%. In that context, the fear that drove central banks to buy gold may abate — and if it does, gold will come down."*

**What Saurabh is doing personally:**
- **Currently:** 50% India equities, 50% Western equities — **zero gold personally**
- Waiting for gold to pull back before buying
- **Target:** When an entry point emerges, put ~1/3 of wealth in precious metals (gold, silver, possibly platinum)
- Marcellus Multi-Asset portfolio already has **10–15% in precious metals**

**His honest caveat:**
> "If I'm wrong and the fear trade continues, gold will keep running. This is a contrarian call with real risk of being wrong."

## 8. Investment Strategy for 2026

**Prediction:** By end of 2026, Indian investors will increasingly question why they are over-concentrated in 4% of the world.

### 8.2 Where Saurabh Is Investing — Marcellus Strategy

**Core Global Holdings:**

1. **TSMC** — Makes chips for the entire world; regardless of which design wins, they win

### 8.3 The Second-Order Beneficiary Framework

| Revolution | First Mover | Second-Order Winner |
|---|---|---|
| Automobile | Ford: 23x | Walmart (built on car mobility): 1,600x |
| WiFi/Internet | Cisco (infrastructure): modest | Netflix (content on WiFi): 500x+ |

**The investment bet:**

- We don't know which AI company becomes the "Netflix"
- So: Buy the infrastructure layer (TSMC, GE Vernova) + companies that already have world-leading positions and will use AI to compound them

### 8.4 The Fear of "Technological Colonialism"

This is Saurabh's deepest concern — and perhaps the most important idea in the entire podcast.

**The British Industrial Revolution pattern:**
- British companies built technology → Sold goods to colonies using military might → India's industries were destroyed → Wealth transferred to Britain

**The AI equivalent today:**
- All IP (hardware + software + biotech + EV) sits in America
- India has **zero relevant IP** in the Third Industrial Revolution
- We are pure **users** of their technology
- As monopoly suppliers of tech → they can squeeze us

> *"China they can't squeeze — China has Rare Earth. We have no such leverage. We have young people — but AI makes young people less valuable. We have no cards to play."*

**China's counterattack capability:**
- Competitive EVs (arguably world's best)
- Rare Earth control
- Multiple competitive LLMs (DeepSeek)

**India's hope:**
- Large, young, educated, English-speaking, tech-literate population
- Cheap broadband connectivity
- These assets make India the natural **back-office for global AI implementation**
- But this is a 3–4 year story. In the interim, there's real pain.

### Key Mental Models from This Episode

1. **Mimetic Desire:** We all want the same things → competition → leaders create scapegoats to redirect conflict

2. **Substitution vs Complementarity:** Every major technology first destroys (candle makers) and then creates (Hollywood). Substitution is fast; complementarity takes time.

3. **The Discount Rate Theory:** High physical threat → high discount rate → focus on today → physical prowess. Low physical threat → long-term thinking → intellectual pursuits.

4. **Second-Order Beneficiaries:** Ford 23x, Walmart 1,600x. Cisco modest, Netflix 500x. The infrastructure enabler rarely captures as much value as the applications built on top.

5. **Home Country Bias:** India = 4% of world. Investing 100% there is irrational over a long time horizon.

6. **Narrative Collapse → Scams:** Scams don't precede narrative collapse — they follow it. When people lose their mental map for the world, they become vulnerable.

7. **Story Telling > Number Dumping:** Markets and customers connect with narratives, not spreadsheets. The companies (and people) who master this will win.
