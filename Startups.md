## 2. Childhood, Bullying & Identity

### The Bullying Years

Deepinder was bullied throughout childhood for three reasons:

1. **Short** — grew about 1.5 feet at age 12 (now 6 feet tall)
2. **Dark-skinned** — mocked for his complexion
3. **Stammered** — couldn't speak fluently

> *"My friends' parents used to tell them: 'Don't be friends with him.' Because smart kids should have smart friends."*

### Grades — 42% in Class 11
- Academically poor performer in school
- Failed to qualify JEE in first attempt — decided NOT to give the exam
- Dad said: "You've already paid the fees, just go give it for practice"
- Opened a roommate's physics book one random afternoon → solved problems instinctively
- Got into **IIT Delhi** with physics carrying him through

### Key Psychological Insight — Validation Through Achievement

At one point, a teacher helped him cheat in an exam → he ranked 3rd → that day, friendships formed, family praised him, a girl liked him.

> **Subconscious realization:** Achievement = People listen to you. People respect you.

This became his hidden driver to build a successful startup — not for fame or money, but so that his **immediate social circle would value what he says**.

## 3. Stammering — The Hidden Superpower

### Facts About His Stammer

- Stammer is **mental, not structural** — "On a good day I can talk about anything"
- It gets worse under nervousness (conferences, public speaking, large crowds)
- **No treatment sought** — "I am done fixing it"
- He physically fell sick 2 days before conferences he'd committed to — his body revolting

### How He Leads Despite Stammering

| Normal CEOs Do | Deepinder Does |
|---|---|
| Town halls, stage speeches | **Has not done a town hall in 7-8 years** |
| Large employee gatherings | **Does Slack town halls — text-based Q&A threads** |
| Investor pitches (group) | **One-on-one investor meetings only** |

### The Peace He Found

> *"Now I'm like — it's fine, the word will come out eventually. And even if I stammer, people will now listen to me. I've earned a little bit of that."*

The fear that nobody would listen to him as a child became the fuel. Now that people listen regardless of stammer — it doesn't bother him.

**Stammer made him:** A **better writer** — compensated with exceptional written communication.

## 5. The "Owner Mindset" vs. "Job Mindset"

### The Core Distinction

| Owner Mindset | Job Mindset |
|---|---|
| There's a critical bug. It's Friday 6 PM. You fix it tonight. | "We'll do it Monday." |
| Takes personal ownership of customer problems | Clocks out when shift ends |
| Treats the company as their baby | Treats it as their employment |
| Gets hurt when things go wrong | Remains detached from outcomes |

### The Test Deepinder Uses

> *"You know there's a big flaw in the system. It will take two hours to fix. It's Friday 6 PM. You say, 'Let's do it Monday.' I would not go home on Friday 6 PM. I would finish that job."*

### Signs Someone Has Shifted from Owner to Job Mindset

- Defers urgent issues to next week
- Doesn't react with urgency when something is broken
- Goes through the motions in meetings (devil's advocate, no solutions)
- Only focused on hours put in, not outcomes created

### His Expectation from Senior Team

> *"I don't want them to think of this as a job. They have to think of this as life. They have to think of this as their own baby."*

> *"The cost of working alongside me is very, very high."*

## 6. No Goals, No Predictions — The Anti-KPI Culture

### Zomato's Radical Approach to Goals

- **No quarterly goals**
- **No annual goals**
- **No OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)**
- **No appraisals with structured timelines**
- **Publicly listed company — still no targets shared at AGMs**

### Why?

> *"If I give somebody a goal, they are going to fall 20% short of it. First of all, how do I know this goal is the right goal? I don't know. Nobody knows."*

> *"If you are a really good person and leader who cares, you would do your best. If I give you a goal, you will calibrate your effort toward that goal — and that may not be your best effort."*

**Deepinder's belief:** Your best effort could be higher OR lower than a set goal. He only wants **best effort**, not goal-calibrated effort.

### How He Judges Performance Without Goals

He reads signals from how people respond to:
- Customer care complaints
- Crisis situations
- Competition
- How much they innovate
- How many risks they take

> *"Someone who is a learner asks more questions than they give answers to."*

## 8. Quick Commerce & 10-Minute Delivery

### The Real Reason 10-Minute Delivery Works

It is **NOT about speeding riders**. It is about **density of stores (dark stores)**.

| Delhi NCR | 400+ Blinkit dark stores |
|---|---|
| Average distance to nearest store | Very short (every neighborhood) |
| Average packing time | 1 minute 40 seconds |
| Distance to cover | ~1 km |
| Time to cover 1 km on a bike | ~8 minutes |

**Result:** 10-minute delivery without any speed pressure.

### What Riders NEVER See

Riders have **NO timer**. The 10-minute countdown is visible only to the customer.

> *"The timer you see — 6 minutes, 7 minutes — only you see it. The delivery partner has no idea what was told to the customer."*

### Response to MP's Claim — "Riders are dying due to 10-minute pressure"

> *"First of all, no timeline is ever given to any of our delivery partners in Zomato or Blinkit."*

Red lights being jumped is a **general India phenomenon** — not caused by Zomato. If the same rider wasn't wearing a Zomato t-shirt, they'd jump the same red lights.

## 9. Gig Workers — Reality vs. Perception

### Scale

| Metric | Number |
|---|---|
| Monthly Active Gig Workers | **7.5 Lakh (~750,000)** |
| Monthly New Hires | 1.5 – 2 Lakh |
| Monthly Exits (voluntary) | 1.5 – 2 Lakh |
| Monthly Terminations | ~5,000 |
| Average Monthly Earning (6 days/week, 8-10 hrs/day) | ₹25,000+ |

### Why People Join

Most workers are **transient population** — factory workers in off-season, students earning pocket money, part-timers. The gig structure lets them come and go as they please.

> *"Students come back from school on a bike, deliver 2 orders on the way home, earn pocket money."*

### What Zomato Provides (That Critics Miss)

- Insurance (accident + health)
- On-time payment
- Freedom — switch ON/OFF whenever
- Dignity — they left worse jobs voluntarily
- Shelters at key zones

### Deepinder's Counterpoint to Critics

> *"People who complain on Twitter that 'you don't pay enough' — how much do you pay your driver? Your maid? Your gardener? These are the same people who left those jobs for THIS job, because THIS job is better."*

### The Bigger Picture — Generational Change

> *"What we are doing is not for them — it's for their children. My dad didn't think about what I should become. He just made sure my basic needs were met. Then I did what I did. Over generations, the country changes."*

### Top 3 Reasons for Rider Termination

1. Marking delivery complete without delivering (food theft)
2. Cash-on-delivery fraud (take the order, don't return change)
3. **Pattern behavior** — same fraud 3+ times despite two prior warnings

## 10. People, Hiring & Firing Philosophy

### Interview Red Flags — Instant Turn-Off

When someone **oversells themselves** → Deepinder switches off completely.

> *"Nobody is awesome, amazing — even in our team, even me. Nobody is perfect. When people sell themselves too much, that means they're going to be messy and political."*

### What He Looks For

**Vulnerability + Humility** — put people in tough spots and see how they respond.

> *"Someone who is a true learner asks more questions than they give answers to."*

### The "Rock Bottom" Method

For high-potential people who aren't breaking through:

- Deepinder **intentionally pushes them hard** — says something harsh, confronts brutally
- Goal: force introspection, open their learning portals
- **4 out of 5 times** → person bounces back stronger, now leads the organization
- **1 out of 5 times** → person doesn't recover → Deepinder carries the guilt

> *"I carry the guilt of those 1 out of 5 people. But statistically, all of that process also made the 4 out of 5 people superbly great."*

### On Firing People

> *"It's the same as the first one. You never get used to it."*

Why? Because he has always worked with them, shared ups and downs — there's **personal attachment**.

### The Process Before Firing (6-9 months)

1. Observe underperformance (3-6 months)
2. Begin feedback conversations
3. Both sides try to change
4. If divergence persists → exit
5. For every person who left, there were **5x** more where the same conversation led to them staying and growing

### Complacency Signals (Post-IPO)

Common pattern in senior employees who made money from IPO:
- Meeting to meeting, playing devil's advocate
- Poking holes in proposals, never providing solutions
- Saying "Let's revisit next week" repeatedly
- Busy, but company made zero progress

> *"You've been busy, bro. But the company made zero progress over the last month."*

### The ₹20 Lakh "Chief of Staff" Experiment

Deepinder put out a job post asking applicants to **pay ₹20 Lakhs** to join as Chief of Staff.

Real purpose: Filter out money-motivated candidates. Those who were bothered by the fee were filtered out. Those who were curious about learning — **17,000 applications** came in, and hiring pipeline from this experiment is still active 1.5 years later.

Nobody paid the ₹20 Lakhs. Those who focused on the payment were rejected. Those who questioned the logic and wrote thoughtfully were hired — and given a 50% salary raise.

## 11. Near-Death Experiences — COVID & Cash Crisis

### COVID — The 45-Day Crisis

Timeline:
- Food delivery business → **completely wiped out during lockdown**
- Revenue: Near zero
- Cost base: Still intact
- **45 days of runway left**
- Burn rate: ~$45 million/month
- Deepinder called all investors → **Zero money raised**

### The Salary Cut Solution

Instead of taking debt, Deepinder asked for **voluntary salary cuts**:

- No flat 30% mandate — purely voluntary
- In return: **2x or 3x ESOP grants** (not announced upfront — just given later as a thank-you)
- 80%+ of ~4,000 employees voluntarily took a cut
- Some took 100% cuts, some 50%, some couldn't afford any

When Zomato went public:
- Those who gave up ₹100 in cash made **30x** that amount in the IPO

> *"We didn't tell people they'd get ESOPs. We asked for voluntary sacrifice. Then when the company did well, we gave them the equity as a thank-you."*

### Deepinder's Debt Phobia

He has **never raised debt** — only equity.

Reason: Deep childhood conditioning from parents who never took loans.

> *"When you take debt, you are cashing into your future. What if you don't make the money in the future? I don't want to answer that question for myself."*

If business can't raise equity: He would **rather shut down** than take debt.

## 15. New Ventures — Lyt Aerospace, Temple & Continuum

### Lyt Aerospace

**The Vision:** Small planes that take off from anywhere in 20-50 meters, land anywhere, at low cost.

**Why?**
- Delhi to Chandigarh: 6 hours by car, 6 hours by flight (airport travel included)
- Idea: Small air strips in every neighborhood → fly point to point
- Could **decentralize cities** — people live 100 km away, commute by air daily
- Wealth distribution to smaller towns
- Factories currently near cities because management is there → if management can fly, factories can move anywhere

**Status:** ₹50 million committed ($25M from Deepinder, $25M from investors including Vamsi Krishna of Vedantu/VY Capital). An early Zomato investor for 12+ years.

> *"If this works, it would completely revolutionize things. If it fails — no problem. Worth trying."*

### Temple (Health Device)

**Origin:** Deepinder has genetic predisposition to:
- Cardiovascular disease (from father)
- Parkinson's (mother has it)

**The Question He Asked:** "What is the leverage point in health — a small change that creates a disproportionately large effect?"

**Hypothesis — Gravity Aging:**

> *"Is gravity one of the overlooked reasons why we age?"*

The logic:
- Our heads are above our hearts
- Gravity means the heart works extra to push blood UP to the brain
- Less blood reaches the brain than optimal
- Over decades, this small deficit accumulates → brain ages → body follows
- **Everything known to be healthy** → increases cerebral blood flow
- **Everything unhealthy** → decreases cerebral blood flow

**Temple Device:**
- A sticker worn on the temple (side of head)
- Non-invasive sensor measuring cerebral blood flow continuously
- Miniaturized version of existing bulky clinical devices (NIRS scanners)
- 5-7 day battery life, waterproof, swim-proof
- Goal: Master biomarker — the single metric that tells you everything about your health state

**Validation:**
- 100+ scientists and researchers from around the world reached out after hypothesis was published
- Harvard Medical School, French universities, Canadian scientists engaged
- 4 contra teams hired to disprove it → all 4 became pro teams

> *"If I don't pay attention to such world-changing ideas, who else do you expect should?"*

### Continuum (Gravity Aging Research)

The research organization behind the gravity aging hypothesis. Funding global studies to prove or disprove the theory.

## 16. Life Philosophy & Deepinder's Mental Models

- Grew up hearing "never take debt"
- Has always been generous — gives freely, never counts
- Feels he "always has enough" regardless of amount

### On Debt

- Family conditioning: Parents never took loans

### On Imposter Syndrome

> *"I got here, was I actually good enough to get here? There is such a huge element of luck in any success."*

He acknowledges it openly but doesn't let it paralyze him.

### On Biggest Fear

> *"My biggest fear is that I stop being intellectually honest with myself."*

If the gravity aging hypothesis is wrong → he will be the **first to publicly say so**, with no shame.

> *"My identity is not tied to this."*

### On Complacency in Others

What pisses him off most:

> *"When somebody's potential is 100 and they are playing at 10 — and they are chill about it. The world owes nothing to you."*

### On India and Wealth

India has a **bad relationship with wealth** — assumption that rich people are evil or stole their money.

> *"Stop having this negative relationship with wealth. Wealth will come to you. Do good work, create value."*

### On Leadership — Athlete Mindset vs. Vulnerable Leadership

Two schools of thought:

| Athlete Mindset | Vulnerable Leadership |
|---|---|
| Show up strong regardless of how you feel | Be open about your bad days |
| The team needs to see confidence | Humanize yourself — say "I'm stressed, sad today" |

**Deepinder's synthesis:**
- With top 3-4 team members → shows vulnerability openly
- With larger groups → puts on the strong face
- But during crises → gathers the top 50, says "We are running out of money. I am stressed. But here's how we get through this."

He shows the feeling **AND** gives the confidence that he won't let the situation beat him.

### On Sleep

- Was terrible sleeper — 5-6 hours a night
- Started "winding down" from 8:30-9 PM: phone in drawer, music on, brush teeth
- Now gets 7.5 hours regularly
- Takes melatonin when needed
- Believes stopping phone stimulation is the key
